# Strategic Communication & Engagement Plan
### GreenCrate (SME Innovator)

## 1. Executive Summary
GreenCrate has the strongest possible asset for a growth campaign — three pilot customers who already use the product — yet it is spreading a thin budget (~£2,000/month, one part-time marketer) across an Instagram account that mostly reaches the wrong people. The fastest route from 3 to 30 customers is not broad awareness but proof-led, targeted selling: turn the pilots into hard cost-and-waste case studies, then use them to win warm conversations with operations and sustainability managers at lookalike regional producers. This plan concentrates effort on a repeatable "case study → targeted outreach → trade-show conversion" loop rather than top-of-funnel content.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operations managers at target producers | Lower packaging cost, reliability, easy switch | High | ROI-led case studies; direct outreach; a simple cost-comparison and a low-risk trial |
| Sustainability managers at target producers | Waste reduction, credible ESG story for reporting | High | Sustainability-impact figures (waste/CO₂ saved); co-branded sustainability content |
| Existing pilot customers | Continued savings; recognition | Med–High | Turn into references & case studies; ask for warm introductions to peers |
| Trade-show organisers & industry bodies | Relevant exhibitors, member value | Medium | Maximise the two shows; seek speaking slots and member newsletter features |
| Regional food & drink press / networks | Local sustainability success stories | Low–Med | One case-study-led pitch per quarter; LinkedIn amplification |
| Logistics / returns partners | Reliable reverse-logistics for the crate model | Low–Med | Keep aligned so the "returnable" promise is dependable in sales conversations |

## 3. Key Messages
**Cut packaging cost and waste at the same time.** GreenCrate's reusable crates replace recurring cardboard spend with a returnable system that pays for itself.

**Proven with producers like you.** Three regional food producers already rely on GreenCrate — this is a working system, not a pilot idea.

**A sustainability win you can actually report.** Switching gives operations a cost case and sustainability managers measurable waste and CO₂ reductions to put in their reporting.

**Low-risk to switch.** Start with a small trial on one product line and scale once the savings are visible.

## 4. Communication Channels
- **Direct, targeted outreach (email + LinkedIn) to named ops/sustainability managers** — the highest-ROI channel for a B2B 3→30 goal; this is where most of the budget's *effort* should go.
- **Customer case studies & a one-page ROI calculator** — the core sales assets; every other channel points back to them.
- **The two annual trade shows, worked hard** — pre-book meetings with target producers; treat each show as a conversion event, not just attendance.
- **Referrals from the three pilot customers** — warm introductions to regional peers convert far better than cold outreach.
- **LinkedIn (company + founder)** — case-study-led posts reach B2B buyers; reallocate effort here from Instagram.
- **Instagram (maintain, don't over-invest)** — useful for brand/sustainability proof and reshares, but it reaches few decision-makers; keep it light.
- *(Budget steer: most of the £2k/month goes to case-study production, a little paid LinkedIn to target producers, and trade-show meeting prep — not broad paid social.)*

## 5. Engagement Timeline

| Phase | Timeframe | Key Activities | Success Indicator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proof & assets | Months 1–2 | Produce 3 pilot case studies + ROI one-pager; build a 60-name target list; tighten messaging | Case studies live; target list + outreach sequence ready |
| Targeted outreach | Months 3–6 | Run outreach to target producers; secure referrals from pilots; book trade-show meetings | 25+ qualified conversations; 6–8 trials started |
| Convert & prove at shows | Months 6–9 | Work both trade shows for meetings; convert trials to contracts; publish results | 10–12 new paying customers; 1 press/feature placement |
| Scale & retain | Months 9–12 | Referral push from new customers; repeat winning outreach; light always-on LinkedIn | Reach ~30 customers; repeatable pipeline established |

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part-time marketer capacity is the bottleneck | High | High | Ruthlessly prioritise the outreach-and-case-study loop; templatise outreach; consider a freelancer for case-study production |
| Effort sunk into Instagram that doesn't reach buyers | Med–High | Medium | Reallocate to direct outreach + LinkedIn; hold Instagram at maintenance level |
| Sales cycle longer than expected (slow switch) | Medium | Med–High | Lead with a low-risk single-line trial; make switching effort-light; quantify payback period upfront |
| Reverse-logistics / returns hiccup damages word of mouth | Medium | High | Nail returns reliability before scaling; pre-empt the objection with pilot data in sales materials |

## 7. Next Steps
1. **Interview the three pilot customers** and turn each into a one-page, numbers-led case study within two weeks.
2. **Build the ROI one-pager** (cardboard spend vs. GreenCrate, with payback period).
3. **Compile a 60-name target list** of regional producers and the ops/sustainability manager at each.
4. **Launch a templated outreach sequence** and ask each pilot customer for two warm introductions.
5. **Pre-book target-producer meetings** for the next trade show rather than relying on stand footfall.
