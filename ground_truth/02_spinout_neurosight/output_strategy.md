# Strategic Communication & Engagement Plan
### NeuroSight (University Spin-out)

## 1. Executive Summary
NeuroSight has a strong technical story but a short clock: ~4 months of runway and a £750k seed round to close, led by founders who are credible scientists yet untested as investor communicators. With effectively no marketing budget, the goal is not visibility for its own sake — it is a tight, evidence-led investment narrative delivered to a small, named list of healthtech investors, warmed by NHS and clinical-partner validation that proves demand. This plan focuses on three things only: a sharp investor narrative and deck, founder credibility-building, and proof points (an NHS pilot letter of intent, the patent, the clinical-validation plan) that de-risk the round.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Healthtech / medtech seed investors & angels | De-risked, scalable return; credible team; regulatory path | High | Warm-intro-led targeted outreach; one-page teaser → deck → data room; founder meetings |
| NHS trust innovation leads | Earlier detection, lower cost, fits existing scan pathways | High | Short clinical briefings; pursue a letter of intent / pilot interest as investor proof |
| Clinical pilot partners (ophthalmology / diabetes leads) | Clinical validation, co-authorship, better patient outcomes | Med–High | 1:1 clinical conversations; offer involvement in the validation study |
| Existing / prospective advisors (clinical, regulatory, commercial) | Mission, equity, reputation | Medium | Recruit 1–2 named advisors to add credibility to the deck |
| University TTO / spin-out support | Successful exit of their IP, follow-on reputation | Medium | Keep aligned; use their investor network and press channels |
| Healthtech media / sector newsletters | A credible AI-in-health story | Low–Med | One milestone-timed announcement once the round is near-close (not before) |

## 3. Key Messages
**We catch sight-threatening disease earlier — on scans the NHS already takes.** No new hardware, no new workflow: NeuroSight adds AI detection to retinal scans clinicians already perform.

**The technology is protected and the demand is real.** A granted patent plus active NHS interest show this is defensible IP solving a problem the health system is actively trying to fix.

**£750k takes us through clinical validation — the last big de-risking step.** The round is tied to a clear, fundable milestone, not open-ended runway.

**A credible team, strengthened where it counts.** Two domain-expert academics and a commercial lead, backed by named clinical and regulatory advisors.

## 4. Communication Channels
- **Warm investor introductions (founders + TTO + advisors)** — by far the highest-conversion channel for a seed round; cold outreach is a last resort.
- **Investor materials: one-page teaser → 10–12 slide deck → simple data room** — the core assets every conversation runs on; must be flawless before outreach starts.
- **Targeted 1:1 meetings with NHS innovation leads** — to convert clinical interest into a letter of intent that de-risks the investment story.
- **Founder LinkedIn (especially the commercial lead)** — free credibility-building; thoughtful posts on the problem and the team, not promotion.
- **A clean, single-page website refresh** — the basic site must clearly state the problem, the proof and the team; it is the first thing a warm intro will check.
- **One earned-media moment, held until the round is near-close** — announce momentum, not intentions; premature press wastes a scarce asset.
- *(De-prioritised: paid ads, broad social, events — wrong audience and no budget pre-raise.)*

## 5. Engagement Timeline

| Phase | Timeframe | Key Activities | Success Indicator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrative & assets | Month 1 | Lock the investment narrative; build teaser, deck and data room; refresh the website; founder pitch practice | Deck + data room investor-ready; founders confident on the 10-min pitch |
| Proof & warm pipeline | Months 1–2 | Secure NHS letter of intent / pilot interest; recruit 1–2 advisors; map and request warm intros to a 25-investor target list | LOI or strong written interest in hand; 15+ warm intros requested |
| Active raise | Months 2–3.5 | Investor meetings; iterate deck on objections; run a tight process with a "first close" target | 8+ investor meetings; ≥2 term-sheet conversations |
| Close & announce | Months 3.5–4 | Convert commitments; legals; one earned-media moment on close | Round substantially committed/closed; announcement live |

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runway runs out before the round closes | High | High | Start the raise on day 1; line up bridge options early; run a disciplined "first close" rather than waiting for a full close |
| Founders under-perform in investor pitches | Med–High | High | Dedicated pitch coaching in week 1; one founder owns the narrative; rehearse the top 20 investor objections |
| Regulatory/clinical-claims overreach (medical AI) | Medium | High | Keep claims evidence-bounded; frame as "validation in progress"; have an advisor sense-check all external materials |
| Story reads as "tech looking for a market" | Medium | Med–High | Lead every conversation with the NHS demand signal and cost case, not the algorithm |
