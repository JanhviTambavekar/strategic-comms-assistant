1. Executive Summary

TidalCarbon's strategic communications plan targets three priority audiences over 36 months: academic researchers, environmental investors, and regulatory bodies. Total 36-month communications budget: £120,000.

2. Stakeholder Analysis

Tier 1 (Primary): Environmental fund managers, climate tech VCs (decision: funding)

Tier 2 (Secondary): University partnership leads, regulatory agencies (decision: credibility, approval)

Tier 3 (Amplifiers): Science communicators, industry analysts (decision: reach)

3. Message Architecture

Core value proposition: "Tidal energy monitoring at unprecedented sensitivity, enabling optimized site selection and real-time performance tracking."

Proof point: Imperial College partnership, pilot deployment data from Port of Rotterdam (verified)

4. 36-Month Timeline & Channel Plan

Months 1-6 (Foundation): Peer publication submissions, website rebuild, LinkedIn cadence (3x/week technical content)

Months 7-18 (Build): 2 industry conference presentations, regulatory stakeholder engagement (UK Environment Agency quarterly briefings), partnership announcement PR

Months 19-36 (Scale): Series A investor roadshow (20 meetings), thought leadership campaign in renewable-energy media, commercial pilot announcements

5. Messaging by Audience

Audience Primary Message Channel
Investment Managers De-risked site selection + performance tracking → faster ROI Investor meetings, whitepapers
Researchers Novel sensing methodology + reproducible datasets Peer publications, conferences
Regulators Compliance-enabling technology for environmental monitoring Stakeholder briefings, consultation responses

6. KPIs & Success Measures

• Month 6: 2 peer-reviewed publications submitted (baseline: 0)
• Month 12: 1 industry conference presentation (2 pilot site communications)
• Month 18: Series A interest from ≥5 climate-tech VCs (verified meeting log)
• Month 36: 3 commercial pilots announced + £2M+ follow-on funding secured

7. Resource Allocation & Budget

• Comms lead (0.5 FTE): £45k/year × 3 = £135k total
• Content creation (design, video): £15k/year

• PR agency support (selective): £4k/year

8. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk: Regulatory timeline delays → Impact: Stakeholder engagement stalls
Mitigation: Pre-position with Environment Agency quarterly; emphasize compliance benefits

Risk: Pilot performance underperforms → Impact: Messaging credibility questioned
Mitigation: Mark pilot results as "validated data" only after independent review

✅ Owner: CEO | Deadline: Week 2

10. Review & Iteration

Monthly check-in: Comms lead + CEO to review publication pipeline, investor interest signals, and regulatory engagement progress. Adjust messaging if pilot data changes or regulatory environment shifts.
