PROJECT BRIEF — "TidalCarbon"

TidalCarbon is a 3-year EPSRC-funded research project at a UK university
investigating how coastal tidal wetlands can be restored to maximise carbon
capture ("blue carbon"). The team has produced strong early results showing a
40% improvement in carbon sequestration using a novel sediment management
technique.

The team wants to communicate these findings to secure follow-on funding,
influence coastal policy, and build partnerships with environmental NGOs and
local authorities. They have limited time, no dedicated comms staff, and a
small dissemination budget of around £5,000. Key outputs include two journal
papers, a public dataset, and an end-of-project policy workshop planned for
month 30.
