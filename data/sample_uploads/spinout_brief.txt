VENTURE BRIEF — "NeuroSight"

NeuroSight is a university spin-out commercialising a patented AI algorithm that
detects early signs of diabetic retinopathy from standard retinal scans. The
company is pre-seed, founded by two academics and one commercial hire, and is
seeking £750k of seed investment to complete clinical validation.

Audiences: healthtech investors, NHS trust innovation leads, and potential
clinical pilot partners. The founders are strong technically but have little
experience pitching to investors or media. They have a basic website, a LinkedIn
presence, and roughly 4 months of runway. They want to build credibility and a
clear investment narrative ahead of a seed round.
