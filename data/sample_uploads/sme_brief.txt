BUSINESS BRIEF — "GreenCrate"

GreenCrate is a 12-person SME that makes reusable, returnable packaging crates
for regional food producers, replacing single-use cardboard. They have proven
demand with three pilot customers and want to grow to 30 customers across the
region within 12 months.

Their audience is operations and sustainability managers at small food and drink
producers. They have a modest marketing budget (~£2,000/month), one part-time
marketer, an active Instagram account, and attend two trade shows a year. They
want to raise awareness of the cost and sustainability benefits and convert more
producers into paying customers.
